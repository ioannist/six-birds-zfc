# Citation Audit

Scope: `paper/references.bib`, excluding the four Tsiokos/SBT sibling-paper entries.

Method: DOI entries were checked against DOI resolution and Crossref metadata where available, then against a publisher/archive/catalog page where practical. Older books and URL-only items were checked against publisher, archive, library, or scholarly-index records. Crossref returns only first pages for some JSTOR-origin DOIs; the full page ranges were retained when confirmed by journal/index records.

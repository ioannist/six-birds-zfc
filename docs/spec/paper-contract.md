# Paper Contract - To Set a Stone with Six Birds

Working title: *To Set a Stone with Six Birds: Hereditarily Finite Closure, Four Completion Commitments, and Forcing as Strict Theory Extension*.

## 1. This Paper IS

- A theorem-led specification of the ZF-track obligation ladder: finite audited packaging first, named completion commitments second, forcing correspondence third.
- A free/purchased ledger for the usual set-theoretic axiom table, with HF closure and finite packaging treated separately from the four completion commitments.
- A conditional architecture paper: infinitary set-theoretic commitments enter through the A-ZF assumption box and the external dependency contract.
- A finite-core paper with a go/no-go gate at the finite Cohen bridge, using Foundations I counting, non-definability, and strict-extension machinery.
- A strict-theory-extension paper: forcing is read through the Cantor-shell non-factorization template and the Foundations I finite forcing spine.
- A Lean-oriented project with anticipated surfaces for packaging, HF closure, rank audit, finite Cohen, and lift/Choice modules.
- A scoped philosophical paper only after the theorem ledger is in place; discussion claims remain meta-grade.

## 2. This Paper IS NOT

- It is not a finite-principle production of ZF or ZFC. The A-ZF commitments mark the infinitary entry points.
- It does not provide a consistency proof for ZFC. Metatheoretic strength remains with the cited classical literature.
- It does not announce novel independence claims. CH examples are model-grade readings of classical Cohen/Kunen results.
- It does not assert that ZFC is the unique or most natural promoted set layer. Typed alternatives and other promoted layers remain acknowledged.
- Its philosophical discussion is meta-grade, quarantined in the discussion section, and never used as theorem support.
